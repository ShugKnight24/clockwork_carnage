# Clockwork Carnage — Brand Kit

Generated by `node scripts/brand/build-brand.mjs`. Edit the script, not the SVGs.

## The mark

A gear broken open into a **C**. Its crimson second hand is stopped at
**eleven seconds** — the eleven seconds the Chronos Engine ran and broke time —
and it escapes through the break in the ring. The cyan minute hand stands at
twelve: the moment before. It says the game in one shape: clockwork, a clock,
time broken open, and the first letter of the name.

The wordmark is hand-lettered, not typeset. The game ships no web fonts, so a
typed logo would change shape on every machine; these letters are geometry and
look the same everywhere. They are condensed and industrial, and every corner
the UI plates chamfer (top-left and bottom-right) is chamfered here too.
**CLOCKWORK** sits over **CARNAGE**; CARNAGE always carries the accent.

## The name: order and chaos, one C each

The name carries its own two marks. **CLOCKWORK**'s C is the gear clock.
**CARNAGE**'s C is that same ring **shattered**: its pieces drift apart and
turn, one slips sideways like a glitched frame, debris flies out of the break,
and violet rift light shows through the cracks — the unknown the Engine let
in. The two big Cs sit on different levels, the second lower and further
right, so the name steps down from order into chaos. Violet (`#9b5cff`) appears
only there, as rift light: it is the brand's one note of the unknown.

The two Cs together also make a mark of their own (`mark-twin`): the gear
clock over its shattered twin.

## One identity, three finishes

Each graphics version gets the same mark and lettering in its own finish, so
the brand follows whichever art style the player picks.

| Version | Finish | Ground | Mark | CLOCKWORK | CARNAGE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Legacy** | Neon line art with glow | Void `#05060c` → `#0d1a38` | Cyan `#22e6ff`, energy `#00ffcc` hand | Cyan neon | Crimson neon |
| **Comic** (default) | Steel under a hard ink line, drop shadow, halftone | Cream `#f3e9cf` | Steel, ink `#04060b`, amber `#ffae3a` pin | Cream | Crimson `#ff2a4a` |
| **Modern** | Machined steel, soft light, no ink | Navy `#0b1422` | Brushed steel | Steel gradient | Crimson gradient |

The second hand is **always crimson** and the minute hand **always cyan**, in
every finish. Those two colours are the brand; everything else is the finish.

## Use

- **Clear space:** one gear-tooth depth (about 8% of the mark's width) on
  every side; keep text and UI out of it.
- **Minimum size:** mark 24 px (favicon version 16 px); horizontal lockup
  180 px wide; stacked lockup 120 px wide.
- **Pick the finish by context:** the in-game title uses the active art style;
  store pages, GitHub and social default to **Comic**, the default art style.
- **Don't:** recolour the hands, stretch or rotate the mark, set the name in a
  font instead of the lettering, put the Comic finish on a dark ground without
  its cream tile, or add effects on top of a finish.

## Voice

Short, dry, a little defiant — the way ARIA talks. Lines that fit the brand:
"Eleven seconds broke time." · "Bend time. Not you." · "One cadet. No backup."
